Our Story

Built by Educators, Shaped by Learners

Stackly started in 2020 with a simple idea: skills shouldn't be locked behind rigid schedules or expensive institutions. What began as a handful of self-paced design courses has grown into a full learning platform trusted by thousands of career switchers, freelancers, and lifelong learners.

  • Courses built and reviewed by working professionals, not just theorists
  • Flexible, self-paced learning that fits around real life
  • A support community that shows up when you're stuck
  • Certificates that actually mean something to employers
